In short

This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking, and loads no content from third parties: fonts and images are served from this server. Personal data is processed only to deliver the site, keep it secure and answer emails.

Server log files

Every time a page or file is requested, the web server records your IP address, the date and time, the requested page or file, the HTTP status code, the amount of data transferred, the referrer URL, and your browser and operating system (user agent).

This is needed to deliver the site and to detect and fix errors and attacks. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is a secure and stable website. Log files are deleted after 14 days at the latest, unless a specific security incident requires keeping them longer.

The site runs on a rented server. The hosting provider processes this data on my behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR).

Cloudflare

Requests to this site pass through Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), which delivers the site and protects it from attacks. Cloudflare processes connection data such as your IP address and the request itself, on my behalf under its data processing addendum.

Data may be processed in the USA. Cloudflare is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: secure and fast delivery of the site.

The site itself sets no cookies. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie if it has to tell people from automated traffic.

Settings stored in your browser

If you switch the theme or the language, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (keys theme and lang). It stays on your device, is never sent to me, and can be deleted in your browser settings at any time. The legal basis is § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, as the storage is strictly necessary for the function you asked for.

Contact by email

If you email me, I process your address, the message and any details you include in order to reply, for example about a job opportunity. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where the message concerns a possible employment or contract, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR otherwise. I delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed, unless legal retention obligations apply.

Links to other websites

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, YouTube, my project sites and others are plain links. No data is sent to these services until you follow a link; from then on, their privacy policies apply.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR). To exercise them, send me an email.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.
